iGOLab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Application: iGOLab — Interactive Geometrical Optics Laboratory (Android)
Developer: Eidan A. Abdullah (Wasit, Iraq)

Data collection and disclosure

The Android application does not require an account and contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Its Android manifest does not request Internet access. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through this application.

Local files and settings

iGOLab processes user-selected optics project files on the device. Exported images and project files are saved locally when requested. App settings and on-device working state remain local. None is uploaded to a developer-operated server. Exported files may be removed in the device's file manager; app-local data can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. The device's optional Android system backup or device-transfer behavior is managed by Google or the device manufacturer, not an iGOLab service.

Permissions and third parties

The app uses Android system file-selection and MediaStore APIs for user-requested import/export. It does not request broad storage permissions or Internet access. No third-party analytics or advertising service is included.

Children and education

iGOLab is a general educational physics tool with no user profiles or social, chat, or messaging features.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the public project's GitHub Issues page.

This policy will be updated before distributing any version with materially different data practices.
